# Azienda — Product Thesis

**Azienda — the AI Business OS. Business applications, run by agents, governed by policy.**

*(Evidence: `docs/research/competitive-analysis.md`, `docs/research/commercialization.md`.
Labels: CONFIRMED / LIKELY / ASSUMED / UNKNOWN per Ekue's rule.)*

## The problem

Businesses run ~27 AI-powered SaaS apps each (BetterCloud 2026 — CONFIRMED), mid-market app
counts up 41% in a year, license utilization ~49% (Zylo 2026 — CONFIRMED). The problem migrated
from *buying too many tools* to *governing and using them*: data disagrees across 4–6 systems,
one business outcome crosses marketing → sales → ops → finance → support through humans or
brittle zaps, the "AI workforce" is four consoles with four bills and four policy models, and
policy lives in six places — none of them in the execution path of an agent's action.

## The thesis

Nobody owns the governed closed loop — **GOAL → CONTEXT → PLAN → POLICY CHECK →
TOOL SELECTION → EXECUTION → OBSERVATION → FEEDBACK → LEARNING** — across departments.
Competitors are assistants that answer, executors trapped in one department, pipes without
policy, or governance sold only to the enterprise. Azienda is the layer that runs the loop:
persistent goals, policy enforced *before* the tool call, durable execution, and an outcome
ledger showing fully-loaded cost per business outcome. **The loop is the product.**

## Who it's for (ASSUMED — validate by interview before it drives spend)

10–100 employee service businesses (home services, logistics/dispatch, agencies, clinics,
professional services) where the owner/COO still touches operations. Buyer: owner/COO/RevOps.
Anti-ICP: solo freelancers, 500+ enterprises, regulated clinical/financial workflows at MVP.

## Positioning

- **Not** a better CRM, not a smarter chatbot, not another automation pipe.
- **The governed agent workforce for the business you already run** — it sits above your
  systems of record (and beside NEXORA ERP), executing cross-department work under your policy.
- **For agencies (incl. GoHighLevel shops):** the governed agent layer on top of GHL, not a
  GHL competitor — policy-gated automation they can resell, with cost-per-outcome reports
  they can show clients.
- **Vertical packs** (logistics first — Ekue's TMS work; LIKELY the vertical where measurable
  agent results appear first) bundle domain schemas, integrations, policies, and evals that
  horizontal platforms can't match.

## Differentiators (ranked, from competitive research)

1. **Governed cross-functional execution for the mid-market** — policy at action time, priced
   for buyers ServiceNow/Sierra/Decagon exclude.
2. **Outcome ledger with transparent unit economics** — fully-loaded cost per qualified lead /
   booked appointment / resolved case / collected invoice, including retries and human review.
3. **Progressive autonomy L0–L5** — per-workflow dial: risk × reversibility × confidence ×
   financial impact × data sensitivity, with approvals and rollback.
4. **Persistent goal state (AGRL)** — goals survive sessions and departments; outcomes reconcile
   against them; the system learns.
5. **Vertical operating packs** — logistics first.

## Business model (ASSUMED tiers — pricing is data, see commercialization §3)

Platform fee + included execution credits + overages + pass-through comms (SMS/voice/email at
provider cost + markup, never bundled). No free tier at launch (unbounded-cost attack surface);
14-day capped trial instead. **Biggest risk: unbounded agent execution cost → budgets, spend
caps, and kill switches are P0, not backlog.**

## What we're NOT building

Another system of record (that's NEXORA / your CRM), another model lab, another Zapier.
We integrate those; we own the loop.
